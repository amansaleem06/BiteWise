Delete your TasteWise account

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This page is for TasteWise users who want their account and associated personal data deleted.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open TasteWise and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again.

How to request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email tastewise2026@gmail.com from the address on your account. Write that you want your TasteWise account deleted. We complete email requests within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your login (Firebase Auth) and profile (name, email, photo, bio).
  • Private data such as nutrition / Your Palette settings, messages you sent, and device notification tokens.

What we keep or anonymize

  • Posts you already shared may stay visible as anonymized plates (author name removed) so restaurant pages and feeds stay intact.
  • We may keep records needed for safety, abuse, or law (for example report logs) for a limited time.

Without deleting your whole account

You can edit your profile and delete individual posts, comments, or photos in the app at any time.
